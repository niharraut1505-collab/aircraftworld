About Aircraft World

Aircraft World is an educational aviation encyclopedia created to help aviation enthusiasts explore aircraft technology and history. Browse fighters, airliners, cargo aircraft, private jets and helicopters, read their specifications, and compare any two side by side.

Specifications are approximate and often differ by variant, so each aircraft name states which variant the numbers describe.
